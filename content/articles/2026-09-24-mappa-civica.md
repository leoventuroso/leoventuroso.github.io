Title: Mappa Civica: a map for my hometown
Date: 2026-09-24 09:00
Slug: mappa-civica

Montereale Valcellina is the small town in Friuli where I grew up, and for a while now I've wanted to give something back to it. So together with my friend Emanuele Nardi I built it a map that brings together, in one place, the public data about the area.

It's called [Mappa Civica](https://mappa-civica.vercel.app/), and it puts a lot on one screen: cycling stress levels,[^lts] hiking trails, points of interest, civil protection data (defibrillators, wildfire history, landslide hazard zones), satellite layers for vegetation, surface temperature and shade, a route planner, and a way for citizens to send in reports.

There's no server behind it: everything runs in the browser. It was built for one small village, but it's designed so that any other comune can take [the code](https://github.com/BeneComune/mappa-civica) and make its own version.

The full story, layer by layer and with how we built it, is in the [newsletter](https://leonardoventuroso.substack.com/p/i-got-tired-of-hunting-for-public).

[^lts]: The same Level of Traffic Stress idea behind [Stress in bici](https://stressinbici.it/) and [LTS-BikePlan]({filename}2026-06-02-lts-bikeplan-published.md).
